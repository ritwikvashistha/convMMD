# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

The convMMD project aims to provide a respectful, inclusive, and
research-focused environment. Participation should make it easier for people
with different backgrounds and levels of experience to discuss software and
scientific questions constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and professional.
- Critique ideas, methods, and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions asked in good faith and explain assumptions clearly.
- Acknowledge contributions and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and research-ethics obligations.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to disagree
  respectfully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, sustained personal attacks, doxxing, plagiarism, or knowingly
publishing another person's confidential information are not acceptable.
Disruptive behavior that prevents technical or scientific discussion may also
be moderated.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces presented as part of the convMMD project. Maintainers may edit
or remove content, close interactions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and repeated behavior.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report concerns privately to `ritwikvashistha@gmail.com`. Include relevant
links and enough context to understand the incident, but do not distribute
unnecessary personal information. Reports will be handled as privately as
practical. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is itself
unacceptable.

This policy follows common open-source community standards, including the
principles promoted by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
